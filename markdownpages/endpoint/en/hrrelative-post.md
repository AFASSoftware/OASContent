Through this endpoint, you create a dossier item (type Family member mutation (Profit) / -41) with a workflow for an employee's manager in InSite. The family member becomes available to the employee when it is approved. The approval can be done through:
- a manual action by the manager;
- an automatic action in the workflow; or
- the use of the UpdateConnector [`KnSubjectWorkflowReaction`](../../apidoc/en/Dossiers%20en%20bijlagen%20en%20workflows#post-/connectors/KnSubjectWorkflowReaction)
